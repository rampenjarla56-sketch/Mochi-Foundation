M.O.C.H.I.Maximizing Opportunities for Community Help & Impact

The Mochi Foundation helps families access essential pet supplies while bringing students, businesses, and community organizations together to support pets and the people who care for them.

Our story

Start with what
you can do.

Mochi began with a simple idea: people should not have to choose between caring for themselves and caring for a pet they love.

Today, we’re growing that idea through student-led projects, community events, supply drives, and relationships with people who want to help.

What Mochi stands for

Mission & Vision.

Pets are family. Support should be accessible.
We’re building a community where practical help can keep more pets healthy, safe, and with the people who love them.

Our mission01

Help families keep their dogs healthy, safe, and cared for.

To help families who need extra support get the basic supplies their dogs need to stay healthy, safe, and cared for.

Pets are part of the family
Our vision02

A community where families can care for themselves and the pets they love.

We want to bring people together so more dogs can stay healthy, supported, and with the families who love them.
